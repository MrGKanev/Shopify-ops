# Независима проверка на Laravel/legacy parity

Този документ е единственото място, което твърди parity между legacy и
Laravel системата. Ред получава verdict, различен от `Not yet audited`,
само след линкнат differential тест, който реално се изпълнява в CI
(`.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `vendor/bin/phpunit -c phpunit.parity.xml`). Когато
едната страна изобщо няма съответна capability, редът е `Verified mismatch
(open)` с `N/A` тест и точните липсващи entry points — няма какво да се извика
диференциално.

Дизайн: [`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-parity-verification-design.md`](superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-parity-verification-design.md).

Verdict е едно от: `Verified match` / `Verified mismatch (fixed)` /
`Verified mismatch (open)` / `Not yet audited`.

проследени cross-cutting mutating/platform workflows, които не са в 72-те

Последно обновено: 2026-09-14, след pilot-а плюс инцидентните находки за
`Comparator::findDuplicates()`, bulk-ignore, Slack/Discord rules mentions,
Slack/Discord notification content, email rules, Shipping Margin Erosion,
Shipped Item Mismatch (×2), Bundle Check, Fulfilled Without Tracking, Fraud
Risk scoring, Discount Abuse, SLA Breaches, Shipment Aging, Carrier
Performance, SS Shipped/Shopify Unfulfilled, Orphan Detector, Active SS
Conflicts, Partial Fulfillment Stalls, Repeat Refunds, Voided Shipments,
Gift Cards, Same IP, Tax Audit, Country Mismatch, Address Scanner,
High-Value No Phone, Email Checker, Tag Policy Audit, Marketing Consent
Audit, Disputes, Return/RMA Tracker, Returned Items Report, Product
Completeness, SKU Duplicates, Zombie Products, Inventory Oversell,
Inventory Aging, Inventory Forecast, Catalog Quality, On-Hold Stall, и Customer LTV по-долу. Systemic finding: the `order_number ?? name`
field-priority reversal (`ssshipped`, `find_orphans`, `itemmismatch`,
`activess`) recurred independently in 4 sibling `Domain\Reports` analyzers —
`RefundTrackerAnalyzer` already had the correct priority, so this wasn't
copy-pasted from one broken source; it's the same *kind* of mistake made
independently each time an order-number matching
index was written. Any future tool review that builds a Shopify-order index
should check this specific pattern first. Скалирането към остатъка следва
risk order-а
от [дизайн документа](superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-parity-verification-design.md#Скалиране-към-всичките-72-инструмента):
mutating/notification-triggering инструменти първи, после read-only reports.

Tool-level closure за последните три registry entries: `run` се покрива от
редовете **Run Audit comparison engine**, **Run Audit inline duplicates panel**
и **bulk-ignore**; `slackrules` се покрива от **Slack notification rules**;
`emailrules` се покрива от двата Email rules реда плюс catalog gap-а по-долу.
Това затваря registry coverage-а, но не превръща open mismatch-ите в приети
продуктови решения.
