## 4. Independent receipt/restock reconciliation discovery

- [ ] Establish an independent receipt source and reliable refund/restock/variant/location joins before proposing a discrepancy detector. Extend existing Return/RMA only if that evidence can identify conflicting restock quantities or reviewed dispositions. Receipt/disposition collection and processing deadlines already exist.
  - Demand: [Disable default “restock item”](https://community.shopify.com/t/please-please-please-disable-default-restock-item-its-so-fustrating/45529?page=2): repeated merchant requests in February–April 2026, through **post #32**. Merchants describe overselling after accidental restocks. Post count is not a vote count.
  - Our app cannot change Shopify admin's checkbox default. Existing Return/RMA already treats valid PROCESSING_REQUIRED, RESTOCKED and NOT_RESTOCKED dispositions with a location as receipt evidence. A mistaken RESTOCKED event could therefore satisfy both sides of a naive check. This proposal needs evidence independent of that event, not another receipt flag derived from it.
  - The refund normalizers currently omit restock type/location details, while the return collector already fetches dispositions/locations. Verify the missing join and independent evidence rather than recollecting existing data. Missing evidence means “unverified”, not proof of error. Keep cancellation/unfulfilled restocks distinct from physical returns.
  - Done when findings link source events and staff can record a reviewed disposition. Start read-only; inventory corrections require a separately reviewed workflow.

## 5. Sales-to-payments reconciliation for returns and exchanges

- [ ] Add an order-level bridge between goods sold/reversed, physical returns, exchanges, store credit, captured payments, refunds, fees and payouts. Explain differences by event date, currency, outstanding payment and non-cash settlement, with source links and CSV export.
  - Demand: [Returns and sales reporting discussion](https://community.shopify.com/c/accounting-and-taxes/anyone-else-notice-that-the-new-returns-system-breaks-all-of/m-p/2497084): original request **30 likes / approximately 40,000 views**, alongside multiple merchant reports. Views indicate attention, not endorsements.
  - Native check: the accepted Shopify staff response of May 14, 2025 announces a timing improvement and distinguishes sales from money movement. Current [sales-report documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/default-reports/sales-report) also distinguishes sales reversals, physical returns and payments. Do not present the old defect as universally unresolved or force sales to equal payouts. Our proposal addresses the remaining reconciliation workflow.
  - Reuse Financial Exceptions, Order Contribution and Return/RMA. Establish reliable transaction/return/exchange joins and coverage first; separate pending, unsupported and unmatched entries. Keep currencies separate unless an explicit conversion basis exists.
  - Done when a refund-only order, an exchange and a store-credit return each have an explainable bridge without double-counted sales or cash. Differences remain review findings, not automatic accounting corrections.

## 6. Supplier-aware replenishment quantities

- [ ] Validate demand for extending Inventory Forecast into deterministic variant/location reorder suggestions using supplier lead time, safety stock, target cover, confirmed inbound quantities, MOQ and case-pack rounding. Proceed beyond discovery only if these inputs are available for our stores.
  - Demand: [Stocky alternative discussion](https://community.shopify.com/t/stocky-alternative-recommendation/553677), continuing through October 2026. Merchants seek affordable purchasing/inventory workflows, but many later replies promote apps; **75 posts does not mean 75 merchant requests**. Evidence is weaker than items 1–5.
  - Native check: [Shopify's Stocky migration guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/inventory/transitioning-from-stocky) already covers purchase orders, replenishment assistance and inventory workflows. Supplier metafields such as MOQ, lead time and case packs are reference data independent of ordering/receiving calculations. Target that calculation gap, not a replacement ERP or a claim that Shopify lacks purchase orders.
  - Reuse the current 30-day Inventory Forecast and SKU aliases. Expose assumptions, unknown supplier inputs and stockout-biased demand; never infer historical availability from current stock. Recommendations remain exportable drafts without automatic purchasing or inventory writes.
  - Done when a merchant can inspect why a quantity was recommended, how inbound stock reduced it and how MOQ/pack size changed it. Missing inputs must prevent false precision.

## 8. Packaging-material usage and replenishment

- [ ] Add a separate supplies ledger for boxes, mailers, inserts and other configured consumables: counted opening balance, receipts, adjustments, explicit package-to-material recipes, observed usage, low-stock thresholds and reorder suggestions. Report usage by material, location and period.
  - Demand: [Packaging-material inventory](https://community.shipstation.com/ideas/add-inventory-tracking-for-packaging-materials-4540/index2.html), **103 votes / 46 replies**, In Consideration. [A renewed boxes request](https://community.shipstation.com/ideas/feature-suggestion-inventory-tracking-for-boxes-4668), **1 vote / 2 replies**, includes July 2025 feedback. Do not add these vote totals as distinct merchants.
  - Native distinction: product stock and package presets do not establish consumable inventory. The original request remains In Consideration, but confirm current account features before building. This differs from product Inventory Forecast and the existing Package Weight comparison.
  - First verify whether source shipment data identifies the actual packaging material. Equal dimensions do not prove the same box, and a printed label does not prove a box was consumed. Keep estimated recipe usage separate from confirmed packing consumption; deduplicate events, support corrections and do not replenish a physical box merely because a label was voided.
  - Done when a physical count can be reconciled to receipts, confirmed usage and adjustments, with estimated/unmapped usage visible. Material costs can later inform contribution scenarios only with an explicit historical basis. Supplies must never become sellable Shopify inventory accidentally.

## 9. Business-rule shipment-split planner

- [ ] Validate an actual store rule unmet by native splitting/routing before prototyping. Only then extend existing remaining-quantity and partial/on-hold data into a read-only split plan for explicit preorder/SKU/tag/quantity/handling rules, with group allocation and cost assumptions. There is no implementation commitment until the residual need is demonstrated.
  - Demand: [Automatically split items into shipments based on rules](https://community.shipstation.com/ideas/automatically-split-items-into-shipments-based-on-rules-4750), **188 votes / 135 replies**, In Consideration. Examples include preorders, different handling methods and quantities exceeding a package. The vote count covers the whole request, not just our proposed subset.
  - Native check: [Order Routing](https://help.shipstation.com/hc/en-us/articles/19061848728347-Introduction-to-Order-Routing) already splits by inventory/location, and [Shopify order-import changes](https://help.shipstation.com/hc/en-us/articles/47307773375387-Shopify-Changes-to-Order-Import) support true split fulfillments. A narrower [multi-carton automation request from April 2026](https://community.shipstation.com/ideas/automation-rules-for-multi-carton-shipments-6012) has **2 votes / 1 reply**. Validate an unmet business rule in our stores; do not rebuild native location routing.
  - Reuse partial/on-hold reports and remaining-quantity data. The new output is a proposed plan, not another stale-order alert. Preserve fulfillment-order/location permissions, item identities and total quantities. Treat packing limits and preorder dates as configured facts, not guesses. Flag conflicting rules and missing stock evidence.
  - Done when a mixed preorder example and a SKU/quantity example produce inspectable plans with no lost or duplicated units. Any later external action must use a verified compatible API and revalidate the current order. [Split/merge API documentation](https://docs.shipstation.com/apis/shipengine/docs/shipping/shipments-split-merge) is not proof that our existing integration/key supports it.

## 12. Payment-authorization exposure before fulfillment

- [ ] Validate a worklist joining payment-authorization status/expiry and remaining collectible amounts to open, held and partially fulfilled orders. Surface orders nearing a verified expiry, expired authorizations with pending shipment, and observed fulfilled orders lacking a successful capture, subject to payment-method policy.
  - Demand: [Hold fulfillment when authorization expires](https://community.shopify.com/t/hold-fulfillment-orders-when-a-payment-authorization-expires/584668), January 19–20, 2026: a merchant describes preorders at risk of being picked after expiry; the discussion includes a suggested Flow workaround. A separate [authorized-but-fulfilled order discussion](https://community.shopify.com/t/why-are-my-facebook-shop-orders-marked-as-authorized-but-not-paid/269741) contains multiple merchant reports. No high-vote count was verified; prioritize only if our stores use delayed capture/preorders.
  - Native check: Shopify already supports [capture at fulfillment, plan-dependent per-fulfillment capture and expiry warnings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/payment-authorization/). Our gap is a fulfillment-linked review queue with exact transaction coverage, not inventing an expiry alert or claiming automatic capture is unavailable. Historical forum advice about unavailable capture-at-fulfillment is outdated.
  - Reuse order timelines, operational issues and the financial bridge in task #5, but do not confuse authorization with a payout or captured funds. Use authoritative transaction expiry/capture evidence rather than order date plus seven days; gateways, extended authorizations and reauthorizations differ. Handle partial captures, failed/pending captures and currencies explicitly. COD, payment terms, gift cards and legitimate deferred-payment workflows require separate policy, not blanket unpaid-shipment flags.
  - Done when a renewed authorization supersedes the old expiry, a partial capture leaves the correct remaining exposure and unknown gateway data stays unverified. Start read-only with review ownership/due dates. No automatic capture, cancellation or fulfillment holds; our queue alone does not block picking in external systems.
